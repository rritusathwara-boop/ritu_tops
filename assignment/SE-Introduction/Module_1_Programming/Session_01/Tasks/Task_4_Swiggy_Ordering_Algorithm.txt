Algorithm: Ordering Food on Swiggy

1. Start.
2. Open the Swiggy app.
3. Enter or select the delivery location.
4. Search for a restaurant or food item.
5. Select a restaurant.
6. Choose the required food items.
7. Add the items to the cart.
8. Review the cart and order amount.
9. Enter or confirm the delivery address.
10. Select a payment method.
11. Make the payment.
12. Check whether payment is successful.
13. If payment fails, choose another payment method and try again.
14. If payment succeeds, place the order.
15. Receive the order confirmation.
16. End.
